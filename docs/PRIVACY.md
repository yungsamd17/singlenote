# Privacy Policy

Singlenote is offline-first and collects nothing.

## Data collected

None. No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking identifiers.

## On your device

Your note and settings stay in the app's private storage on your device. They are stored unencrypted (plaintext database and preferences): anyone with access to your unlocked device can read them. Automatic app backup is disabled. Uninstalling the app removes its private data. Files you explicitly export are stored wherever you choose and remain there until you delete them.

## Copy and share

Copy puts plain text on the system clipboard — the system may show a preview, and it may linger in keyboard history. Share sends plain text to the app you choose. Archive export writes unencrypted JSON or plain-text files to the location you select; a document provider may store or synchronize those files according to its own settings. See [ARCHIVE.md](ARCHIVE.md).

## Permissions

Notifications (optional): only to pin your note so it stays visible.

## Network

Fetching public release notes and checking for updates contacts GitHub, and only when you open them. No accounts, no analytics, nothing else is ever sent anywhere.
