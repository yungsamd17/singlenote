# Privacy Policy

Singlenote is offline-first and collects nothing.

## Data collected

None. No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking identifiers.

## On your device

Your note and settings stay in the app's private storage on your device. They are stored unencrypted (plaintext database and preferences): anyone with access to your unlocked device can read them. Auto-backup is disabled, so nothing is copied to your Google account — uninstalling the app deletes everything.

## Copy and share

Copy puts plain text on the system clipboard — hidden from the preview on Android 13 and later, but it may linger in keyboard history. Share sends plain text to the app you choose.

## Permissions

Notifications (optional): only to pin your note so it stays visible.

## Network

Fetching public release notes and checking for updates contacts GitHub, and only when you open them. No accounts, no analytics, nothing else is ever sent anywhere.
